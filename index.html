Anuj Vaishnav, PhD

Team Lead at Bloomberg · Electronic Trading

I build and optimise computer systems. Today that means high-performance distributed systems for fixed income trading. Before that, latency engineering at AMD, a startup, and a PhD at the University of Manchester on resource management and FPGA virtualisation, where I designed FOS, an operating system for FPGAs (watch the demo).

Across FPGAs, RTL, compilers, networking and distributed systems I keep meeting the same problem: the hard part is not solving the bottleneck, it is finding which one matters. Lately I have been writing about whether AI agents can learn to do the same.
